Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does house cleaning cost with Clean & Shiny?

Standard residential cleaning starts at $180 and deep cleaning starts at $280. The final price depends on the condition, size and distance of your location. Fill out the quote form or call (773) 848-2035 for an exact price.

What areas do you serve?

We clean homes and businesses in Chicago and the surrounding suburbs, within about 40 miles.

What is the difference between regular cleaning and deep cleaning?

Regular cleaning is ongoing maintenance: bathrooms and kitchen washed down, surfaces cleaned, dusting, floors cleaned and carpets vacuumed. Deep cleaning goes further: bathroom walls, trim, windows, cabinets, light fixtures and vents, plus kitchen cabinets and every appliance.

Is cleaning inside the oven and refrigerator included?

With deep cleaning, oven, refrigerator and cabinet interiors are available at an additional cost. Move-in and move-out cleaning includes the refrigerator interior, ovens, and cabinets inside and out.

Do you clean offices and new construction?

Yes. In addition to residential cleaning, we offer commercial cleaning and post-construction cleaning. Request a quote with the details of your space.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. Clean & Shiny is fully insured and bonded, so your home and belongings are protected while we work.

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 6am to 5pm.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, Zelle, Cash App, card and check.

How do I get a quote?

Complete the quote form on this page with your home's size, condition and preferred dates, or call (773) 848-2035 for a phone consultation.
